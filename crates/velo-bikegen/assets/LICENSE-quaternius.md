# quaternius_bicycle.obj

Low-poly bicycle mesh from the **"LowPoly Public Transport"** pack by
**Quaternius** (https://quaternius.com/), mirrored on OpenGameArt:
https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-public-transport

License: **CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)** — confirmed on
both the Quaternius pack page and the OpenGameArt listing. No attribution
required; credited here out of courtesy.

The file is the unmodified `Bicycle.obj` from the pack's OBJ folder. It is
converted to a tinted, face-shaded GLB at runtime by
`crates/velo-bikegen/src/obj.rs`.
